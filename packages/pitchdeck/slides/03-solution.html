What is Lookout?

One rule. One incident.
One person who can act.

01DefineChoose a source, describe the event, and assign a recipient.
02CheckAssess what the camera shows and review a candidate with evidence.
03AlertOpen one incident and notify the assigned person.
04RespondInspect the clip, acknowledge, dismiss, or resolve.

Acknowledge means “I’m handling it.” Resolve means the response is complete.

This is the agreed end-to-end product flow, not a claim that every connection is deployed. Rules are specific to a source and recipient. Candidate review precedes automatic alerting. Repeated observations of the same event should enrich the open incident rather than create repeated initial alerts. Acknowledgment stops further paging; a person resolves or dismisses the incident separately. Live AI assessment, target-device background push, and cloud clip retention still need end-to-end proof; describe the flow as the product direction until those steps pass a real run.
