Lookout AI / The problem

Cameras record.
Someone still has to watch.

When no one is watching, an incident can go unnoticed and help can arrive too late.

0:00–0:40. Start with the problem before introducing the product. Cameras capture footage, but someone still has to watch it and recognize when help is needed. Imagine a fall on a camera feed while the person responsible is occupied elsewhere. The recording exists, but the incident can go unnoticed and the response can be delayed. That is the problem we want to solve. Briefly introduce the team, then move to Meet Lookout. Do not claim measured response times or imply that all existing camera systems lack alerts.
